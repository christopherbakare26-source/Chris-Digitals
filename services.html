Services

WordPress work that solves a business problem.

Eight focused services covering the full lifecycle of a WordPress site — from the first build to the maintenance that keeps it healthy.

01

WordPress Website Design

A complete site built from scratch around your services, your audience and the action you want visitors to take. Clean design, sensible structure, and a layout that guides people towards getting in touch.

  • Structure and page planning
  • Custom design in your brand colours
  • Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts
  • Contact forms and enquiry flow
02

WordPress Website Redesign

Your site works, but it looks dated or doesn't convert. I rebuild the design and layout while keeping your content, URLs and SEO intact — so you gain a better site without losing what you've built.

  • Audit of the current site
  • New layout and visual hierarchy
  • Content migration without broken links
  • Improved calls to action
03

Divi Website Design & Customization

Divi is powerful, but a badly built Divi site becomes impossible to edit. I build Divi and Divi 5 sites with a clean module structure your team can actually work with after handover.

  • Divi and Divi 5 builds
  • Custom module styling
  • Reusable global templates
  • Editable, documented layouts
04

Elementor Website Design

Elementor and Elementor Pro builds that load quickly and stay consistent. I keep the widget count sensible and the styling systematic, so the site doesn't slow down as you add pages.

  • Elementor Pro builds
  • Global styles and templates
  • Theme Builder headers and footers
  • Performance-conscious structure
05

Landing Page Design

A single page with one job: turn a visitor into an enquiry. Built for ad campaigns, service launches or a focused offer — with a clear message, proof, and an obvious next step.

  • Single-goal page structure
  • Persuasive section flow
  • Lead capture form
  • Mobile-first layout
06

WooCommerce Website Development

An online store that's straightforward to buy from and straightforward to manage. Product setup, cart and checkout configured and tested on real mobile devices before launch.

  • Product and category setup
  • Cart and checkout configuration
  • Payment and shipping options
  • Order flow testing
07

Website Maintenance & Bug Fixes

Something broke and you're not sure why. I diagnose the actual cause — plugin conflict, theme update, broken form — fix it, and tell you what happened so it doesn't repeat.

  • Layout and styling fixes
  • Plugin and theme conflict resolution
  • Form and email delivery repair
  • Core, theme and plugin updates
08

Website Improvements & Optimization

The site is fine, but it could be better. Faster loading, tighter spacing, clearer forms and stronger calls to action — the changes that quietly lift enquiries without a full rebuild.

  • Speed and loading improvements
  • Spacing and typography refinement
  • Form and conversion optimisation
  • Image and asset optimisation

Q

Do you work with existing WordPress sites?

Yes — most of my work is improving sites that already exist. I'll review what's there and tell you honestly whether it needs a fix, a redesign, or a rebuild.

Q

Which page builder will you use for my site?

Whichever fits your project. If you already use Divi or Elementor, I'll stay in that system. For a new build, I'll recommend the option that keeps the site fastest and easiest for you to maintain.

Q

Can you work with my designer or agency?

Yes. I regularly work alongside designers and agencies, taking the WordPress build while someone else owns the brand and strategy.

Q

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo and brand assets if you have them, any existing content, and a clear idea of the goal. If you don't have content ready, we can plan the structure first and fill it in as we go.

Q

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes — updates, backups, bug fixes and small content changes can be handled on an ongoing basis after launch.

Not sure which service you need?

That's what the discovery call is for. Describe the problem and I'll tell you what it actually takes to solve it.
